Duolingo or Easy Peasy English: different jobs, not rivals

Duolingo guides learners through a course with several exercise types. Easy Peasy English focuses on Russian-language vocabulary cards and scheduled reviews. For many learners, using both makes sense.

No “best app” rankingChecked 19 August 2026

The short answer

Choose by task.

Use a course when you want a sequenced path across different language skills. Use a separate flashcard trainer when you want to retain specific words and phrases from your own life.

When Duolingo is the logical choice

Choose it when you want the app to determine the sequence of topics and exercise types. Duolingo's official description says course units are organised around communicative goals and combine vocabulary and grammar for those goals.

When Easy Peasy English is useful

  • you want to save vocabulary from work, travel, a book or a video;
  • you need separate EN → RU and RU → EN practice;
  • you want to see the exact cards due today;
  • you prefer ready-made content designed for Russian-speaking learners;
  • you want a calm flashcard workflow rather than a course path.

How to use both

  1. Use the course for sequenced practice.
  2. Select only the expressions you genuinely want to use.
  3. Add them to Easy Peasy English with clear context.
  4. Complete the short vocabulary queue separately from the lesson.

The course provides breadth; the cards maintain access to selected vocabulary.
