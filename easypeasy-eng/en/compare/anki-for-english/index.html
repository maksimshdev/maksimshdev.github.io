Anki or Easy Peasy English: flexibility or a ready workflow

Both tools can schedule flashcards with FSRS. The important difference is how much you want to configure and whether ready-made content for Russian-speaking learners matters to you.

No affiliate linksChecked 19 August 2026

The short answer

Anki is a configurable toolkit. Easy Peasy is a ready product workflow.

Choose Anki for custom note types, templates and detailed control. Choose Easy Peasy when you want a Russian-language mobile app with ready-made English decks and minimal setup.

When to choose Anki

  • you are building a specialised or complex knowledge base;
  • you need custom fields, templates or formatting;
  • you want to control presets and scheduler parameters;
  • a desktop workflow and broad ecosystem are important.

The official Anki manual describes cards as question–answer pairs and supports creating your own material when explanation and context matter.

When to choose Easy Peasy English

  • you need ready content for Russian-speaking learners;
  • you do not want to configure note types and templates;
  • you prefer a calm mobile interface;
  • you want separate EN → RU and RU → EN practice;
  • a focused vocabulary trainer is enough.

Can you use both?

Yes. You might keep specialist work or exam cards in Anki and use Easy Peasy for ready-made spoken-English topics. Avoid duplicating the same vocabulary in two daily queues without a reason: it adds workload but not necessarily better practice.
