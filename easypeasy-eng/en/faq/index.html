FAQ

Questions about Easy Peasy English

Straight answers about the app's audience, spaced repetition, offline access, personal cards and data.

Updated October 2, 2026
SectionsProductLearningPremium subscriptionAccess and data

Product

What is Easy Peasy English?

A Russian-language mobile trainer for Russian-speaking learners of English. It includes themed decks, personal cards, two study directions and FSRS scheduling.

Is it a complete English course?

No. The app helps retain vocabulary, but it does not replace a structured grammar course, a teacher or real conversation.

Where can I download it?

From the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Google Play for Android.

Learning

What is FSRS?

A spaced-repetition algorithm. It uses each card's rating history to decide when the card should return. Read the plain-English FSRS explanation.

Why are there four answer buttons?

They distinguish a forgotten, difficult, normal or easy answer. This is not a judgement of the learner; it is a signal for the next interval.

Why are EN → RU and RU → EN separate?

Recognising a ready-made English phrase and actively producing it from a Russian prompt are different skills. Separate progress prevents strong recognition from hiding weak active recall.

Can I add my own words?

Yes. You can create personal cards and edit ready-made cards for your account. Creating and importing your own decks are included in Premium; anything you have already created stays with you.

How much ready-made content is included?

The current content database contains 83 visible ready-made decks, 2,620 unique cards and 12 categories. They are built from 69 source content decks; thematic and combined collections do not increase the unique-card count.

Premium subscription

Is the app paid? What is free?

The app is free to download. Free access includes 13 ready-made decks, unlimited reviews and up to 30 new cards per day. Premium unlocks all decks, Today, Favourites and creating your own decks. The first 3 days of Premium are free. The App Store shows the price for your country before purchase. Premium subscriptions are currently available in the App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac). The Android version does not yet have subscriptions: everything remains available as before.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your Apple ID settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Easy Peasy Premium → Cancel Subscription. Do this at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I enter a promo code?

On the Premium screen, tap “I have a promo code” and enter the code in Apple’s window.

How do I restore purchases?

On the Premium screen, tap “Restore Purchases”. Sign in to the same account you used for the purchase.

Access and data

Can I study offline?

Yes. After sign-in, sessions, decks and progress are available without a network connection. Internet access is required for sign-in and synchronisation between devices.

How do I contact support?

Email easy.peasy.msh@gmail.com or use the support page. Support replies are normally in Russian.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. You can also follow the web deletion instructions.

Where can I read about data processing?

See the Privacy Policy. Use of the app is governed by the Terms of Use.
