FAQ

Questions about Easy Peasy English

Straight answers about the app's audience, spaced repetition, offline access, personal cards and data.

Updated 19 August 2026
SectionsProductLearningAccess and data

Product

What is Easy Peasy English?

A Russian-language mobile trainer for Russian-speaking learners of English. It includes themed decks, personal cards, two study directions and FSRS scheduling.

Is it a complete English course?

No. The app helps retain vocabulary, but it does not replace a structured grammar course, a teacher or real conversation.

Where can I download it?

From the App Store for iPhone and Google Play for Android.

Learning

What is FSRS?

A spaced-repetition algorithm. It uses each card's rating history to decide when the card should return. Read the plain-English FSRS explanation.

Why are there four answer buttons?

They distinguish a forgotten, difficult, normal or easy answer. This is not a judgement of the learner; it is a signal for the next interval.

Why are EN → RU and RU → EN separate?

Recognising a ready-made English phrase and actively producing it from a Russian prompt are different skills. Separate progress prevents strong recognition from hiding weak active recall.

Can I add my own words?

Yes. You can create personal cards and edit ready-made cards for your account.

How much ready-made content is included?

The current content database contains 74 visible ready-made decks, 2,281 unique cards and 11 categories. They are built from 66 source content decks; thematic and combined collections do not increase the unique-card count.

Access and data

Can I study offline?

Yes. After sign-in, sessions, decks and progress are available without a network connection. Internet access is required for sign-in and synchronisation between devices.

How do I contact support?

Email easy.peasy.msh@gmail.com or use the support page. Support replies are normally in Russian.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account. You can also follow the web deletion instructions.

Where can I read about data processing?

See the Privacy Policy. Use of the app is governed by the Terms of Use.
