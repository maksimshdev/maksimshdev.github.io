Clear positioning

A vocabulary trainer, not a miracle promise.

Easy Peasy English helps Russian-speaking learners retain useful words and phrases. It works best alongside reading, listening, a course or real conversation.

A good fit if you want to

  • learn vocabulary for real situations;
  • start with ready-made Russian–English decks;
  • study in short sessions;
  • avoid configuring an algorithm manually.

It does not try to replace

  • a complete grammar course;
  • conversation with a teacher;
  • pronunciation practice with people;
  • systematic work on every language skill.

Short answers

Before your first deck.

The method, offline access, personal cards and the app's limits.

Who is the app for?

Easy Peasy English is designed for Russian-speaking learners. The app interface, explanations and built-in translations are in Russian.

What is FSRS?

It is a spaced-repetition algorithm. It uses each card's rating history to calculate the next useful review time separately for both study directions.

Can I study without internet access?

Yes. After sign-in, study sessions, ready-made decks and progress are available offline. A connection is needed for sign-in and synchronisation.

Can I add my own words?

Yes. You can create your own cards and edit ready-made cards for your account. Creating and importing your own decks are included in Premium; anything you have already created stays with you.

Is the app paid? What is free?

The app is free to download. Free access includes 13 ready-made decks, unlimited reviews and up to 30 new cards per day. Premium unlocks all decks, Today, Favourites and creating your own decks. The first 3 days of Premium are free. The App Store shows the price for your country before purchase. Premium subscriptions are currently available in the App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac). The Android version does not yet have subscriptions: everything remains available as before.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your Apple ID settings: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Easy Peasy Premium → Cancel Subscription. Do this at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I enter a promo code?

On the Premium screen, tap “I have a promo code” and enter the code in Apple’s window.

How do I restore purchases?

On the Premium screen, tap “Restore Purchases”. Sign in to the same account you used for the purchase.
