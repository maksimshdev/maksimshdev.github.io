Short answer

Easy Peasy English is…

a Russian-language mobile app for Russian-speaking learners who want to retain useful English words and phrases with active recall and FSRS spaced repetition.

Verified facts

Platforms
iOS and Android
App language
Russian
Ready-made content
74 visible ready-made decks built from 66 source content decks, with 2,281 unique cards across 11 categories
Directions
English → Russian and Russian → English, with separate progress
Method
active recall and FSRS spaced repetition
Offline access
study, decks and progress work offline after sign-in; internet is needed for sign-in and synchronisation
Personal material
users can create cards and edit ready-made cards for their account
Developer
Maksim Shalotenko

How a session works

  1. Choose a topic, a ready-made deck or your own cards.
  2. Try to recall the answer before revealing the translation.
  3. Rate the recall using one of four options.
  4. FSRS calculates when the card should return.

This creates a personal review queue for today without requiring users to manage every card's calendar.

What the app does not promise

Easy Peasy English is a vocabulary trainer. It does not claim to replace a teacher, a complete language course, a grammar programme or conversation with real people.
